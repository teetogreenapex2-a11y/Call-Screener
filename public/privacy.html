Privacy Policy

Call Screener, from Tee to Green Golf. Last updated October 12, 2026.

Call Screener answers your incoming calls with an AI assistant, decides whether the caller is someone you want to talk to, and rings your phone only for approved calls. This policy explains what information the app and its service handle, who helps process it, and the choices you have.

Information we handle

  • Callers' phone numbers and words. When someone calls your screening number, we receive their phone number and what they say. Twilio turns their speech into text, and that text is used to decide whether to put the call through. The AI assistant tells every caller it is an AI at the start of the call.
  • Your call list. We keep a list of your 100 most recent calls: the caller's number, a one-sentence summary, the AI's category (for example legit or sales), and the outcome.
  • Voicemail. If you switch voicemail on, a caller who is not put through can leave a message. We keep the recording (stored with Twilio), a text transcript, and a link to play it.
  • Your contacts. If you allow contacts access, the app copies the names and phone numbers in your phone's contacts to your account. We use them to ring those people straight through and to show their names in your call list. The app only uploads them to your account and does not use them for anything else. You can see and remove them in the app at any time.
  • Account and device information. Your private access key, your phone number, your chosen settings (voicemail, where calls ring, ring time), a push-notification token for your phone, and a count of screened calls per month.
  • Microphone audio during a call. When you answer a call in the app, audio is carried between you and the caller. We do not record answered calls.

Who helps us process it

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising. We use these service providers to run the service:

  • Twilio carries calls, converts speech to text, and stores voicemail recordings.
  • Anthropic runs the AI model. It receives the caller's number, the text of what they said, and instructions about you (such as your name and the kinds of calls you want), so it can decide what to do.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers the signal that wakes your phone for an incoming call.
  • Vercel hosts the service and Upstash stores your settings, contacts and call list.
  • ntfy sends optional push alerts about calls to the topic name shown in your app. Anyone who knows that topic name could read the alerts, so keep it private.

How long we keep it

  • Your call list keeps the last 100 calls.
  • Voicemail recordings and transcripts stay until you ask us to delete them or you close your account.
  • Your contacts stay until you remove them or close your account.
  • All data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Your choices

  • Remove any contact, clear your call list, or switch voicemail off in the app.
  • Revoke the contacts, microphone or notification permission in your phone's settings at any time. Ring-through for contacts and answering calls in the app will stop working.
  • To have your account and all data deleted, email us at the address below and we will do it.

Recording and local laws

Voicemail is recorded after a beep. Laws about recording calls differ by place, and you are responsible for using the voicemail feature in line with the laws that apply to you.

Children

Call Screener is not directed to children under 13 and is intended for adults.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Material changes will be announced in the app.
